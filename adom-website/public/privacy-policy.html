Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026  |  Notice version: 2026-11-11-v2

This notice explains how ADOM Refrigeration & Air Condition Workshop ("ADOM", "we", "our" or "us") handles information submitted through this website.

Information you may provide

If you contact the workshop, you may provide your name, telephone number, area or location, requested service, vehicle details (for automotive AC enquiries), and a description of the issue. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Why information is used

Information is used to understand your service request, contact you, clarify the work required, arrange an assessment and provide customer support. We do not sell enquiry information.

How enquiries are sent

When you submit the website enquiry form, your details are sent over an encrypted connection to the website's server and stored in a private lead database that is not publicly accessible. We also record the time you gave consent, the version of this notice you agreed to, and the page you submitted from.

Enquiries are first reviewed by the independent web and lead-management partner who operates this website. That partner may contact you to understand your request and then pass it on to ADOM so the workshop can follow up. Your reference number lets you and the workshop identify your enquiry.

If the online form cannot save your enquiry, the website will tell you so and may offer a WhatsApp message you can choose to send yourself. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own privacy terms apply.

Before launch: the business owner must confirm this section matches the live data flow and list the actual hosting, database and email providers, the retention period and the contact route for privacy requests. This notice must be updated whenever analytics or other services are added.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with the lead-management partner and with workshop personnel who need it to respond to your request, and with technical providers that host or deliver this website's services. If you choose to send an enquiry through WhatsApp, information is processed by WhatsApp under its own terms. We do not authorize unrelated use of your enquiry details.

Retention and security

Stored enquiries are protected by authenticated, role-restricted access, encrypted connections and abuse controls. Messages sent through WhatsApp or email may remain in those services according to their settings and retention practices. Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for legitimate business recordkeeping, and can be deleted on request. No website can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may choose not to submit an enquiry. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have sent to ADOM, contact the workshop using the details below and quote your reference number. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business recordkeeping.

Children

This website is intended for people seeking repair and maintenance services. Do not submit a child's personal information unless you are their parent or guardian and it is necessary for the enquiry.

Third-party links

This website may link to WhatsApp, Google Maps or other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
